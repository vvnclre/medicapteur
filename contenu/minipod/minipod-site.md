The 
Podocapture
app offers an innovative and intuitive solution for baropodometric examination, making it easier to read plantar pressures.
Designed to work seamlessly on Android tablets and smartphones, it can be downloaded for free from the Play Store.
Podocapture provides a clear and concise visual representation of pressure exerted on the soles of the feet.
Healthcare professionals can quickly obtain precise information, including peak pressure points, average pressures, impulse zones, distribution of weight between left/right and front/back, and areas at risk for diabetic foot complications.
With its intuitive and ergonomic interface, Podometry becomes an educational tool that facilitates dialogue and patient care.
Communicating with prescribers or colleagues is a breeze: Podocapture offers a one-click automatic report generation feature.
Reports can be printed using your Wi-Fi-enabled printer or simply digitally transferred to the network of your choice, such as SMS, email, WhatsApp, Google Drive, and more.
Podocapture is the only application on the market to natively offer diabetic foot scoring (preventive detection of ulceration risk levels).
If the risk is confirmed (scoring above 2), the application automatically highlights areas that require offloading.
Individuals with grade 2 and 3 diabetes are particularly susceptible to foot ulcers due to neuropathies and poor blood circulation.
Podocapture allows for monitoring diabetic feet and establishing the connection between plantar pressures and ulceration risk zones.

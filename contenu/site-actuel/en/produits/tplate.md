## Texte de la page (verbatim, onglets dépliés)

```text
T-PLATE foot pressure plate

3 types   : USB , USB+Wifi or USB+Bluetooth

Description

Slim, smart, simple, ease an improved diagnosis process.

T-PLATE foot pressure plate is the most accurate and cost-effective way to identify the plantar pressure profile.

Every day 15,000+ T-Plate scan tens of thousands of feet. 300+ research articles support Medicapteurs foot pressure sensors

A modern exam

T-Plate provides an easy and efficient patient follow up (up to 4 exams comparison)

Real Time display

Immediate storage or load of recorded exams

Visual provides attractive screens to facilitate patient communication and understanding

Options :

Drive your pressure plate  from your Ipad, tablet or smartphone with Podocapture app download it for free on Googleplay or Apple store

Altuglas positionner                      Carrying bag

Download T-plate brochure

T-Plate software

Static mapping with center of pressure, Max, Average pressure calculations and weight distribution pattern
Wide range of display (Thermographic, Isopressure, 3D…)
Measuring possibilities (length, angle, pressure, area…)
1:1 printing report
Foot posture Index copyright Anthony Redmond
Diabetic foot ulcerations (DFU) : automatic calculation of the DFU level of risk + automatic display of the risk zones.

Movie and frame by frame mode
Global Dynamic view
Gait line Display
Calculations and curves
Automatic diabetic foot report
CPEI center of pressure excursion index
Print 1:1
Statokinesigram and Stabilogram
Instability International parameters
(Altuglass positioner in option)
```

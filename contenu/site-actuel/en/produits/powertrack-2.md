```text
POWERTRACK

All in one pressure sensors treadmill, removable sensors for walkway use

PowerTrack is the result of 37 years of developments in the field of baro-podometry.
Unique on the market, PowerTrack mix our professionnal pressure sensors to a strong gym
treadmill. Powertrack allows various type of assessments: from fall risk patient to top athlete.

Software release 10 , Unmatched features :

– Thermographic view , isopressure, 3D, histograms, diagrams,
– Exclusive features : FPI (Foot Posture Index), CPEI (Center of Pressure Excursion Index),
Diabetic foot pressure Index , Ulcerations level of risk by David Armstrong.
– Sway index , COP Butterfly, symetry, balance, pace, angles etc…
– Customizable clinical assessment.
– Customizable reports, PDF export.
– Database importation of previous Medicapteurs software
– Vidéo synchronisation with to 2 cameras.

Download the provisional brochure
```

## Texte de la page (verbatim, onglets dépliés)

```text
XT PLATE

A cost effective and light foot pressure plate , USB , USB+Wifi or USB+Bluetooth models

Description

Slim, smart, simple, ease an improved diagnosis process

T-PLATE foot pressure plate is the most accurate and cost-effective way to identify the plantar pressure profile.

Traditional tools cannot provide the same information as the T-Plate. It will increase your credibility and most of all help you make the good diagnosis.

A modern exam

Provide an easy and efficient patient follow up (up to 4 exams comparison)

Real Time display

Immediate storage or load of recorded exams

Visual provides attractive screens to facilitate patient communication and understanding

Characteristics

Mechanical Features
Size (Lenght / Width / Height)	565x612x22 mm
Thickness	8 mm
Weight	3,1 kg
Material	Plexyglas
Electronic Features
Sensing area	400 mm * 400 mm
Sensors	Resistive
Sensors size	10 mm * 10 mm
Number of sensors	1 600
Acceptable temperature	from 0 °C to + 60 ° C
Min to Max pressure per sensor	from 0,4 N to 100 N
Sampling rate	100 Hz / 150Hz Wifi
Plateform computer interface	USB or Wifi
Power supply	USB wire / Lith battery
Required operation system	Windows Seven, 8,10

Options

T-Plate is also available in WIFI connection with batteries

T-Plate-WIFI

Altuglass positioner (60° reference)

Carrying bag

Download T-Plate brochure

Softwares
T-Plate software

Static analysis

Static mapping with center of pressure, Max, Average pressure calculations and weight distribution pattern
Wide range of display (Thermographic, Isopressure, 3D…)
Measuring possibilities (length, angle, pressure, area…)
1:1 printing report
Foot posture Index copyright Anthony Redmond
Diabetic foot ulcerations level of risk and zones to offload pressure.

Dynamic analysis

Movie and frame by frame mode
Global Dynamic view
Gait line Display
Calculations and curves
Automatic diabetic foot report
CPEI center of pressure excursion index
Print 1:1

Postural analysis

Statokinesigram and Stabilogram
Instability International parameters
(Altuglass positioner in option)

Videos

Autosynchronization between pressure and video
Angle and length measuring tool
```

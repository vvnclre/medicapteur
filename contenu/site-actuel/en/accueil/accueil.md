Our Products

Company overview

For 40 years, MEDICAPTEURS has been a partner in research worldwide, with over 300 published studies in fields such as podometry, posturology, biomechanics, and diabetology.

Who are we ? What we do ?

Since 1985, Medicapteurs has been designing and manufacturing foot pressure sensors and devices for measuring plantar pressure.

As a world leader in plantar pressure measurement for many years, we provide a complete range of products designed to meet the needs of professionals in medicine, rehabilitation, sports, pharmacy, retail, footwear, and orthotics.

More than 20,000 users worldwide—including shoe and orthotic manufacturers, podiatrists, physicians, biomechanists, physiotherapists, diabetologists, and foot care specialists—use our devices and software every day to enhance their expertise and improve patient care.

Medicapteurs has built a strong reputation by supporting numerous research teams and contributing to more than 300 published scientific articles.
